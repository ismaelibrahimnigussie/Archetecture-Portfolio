ABOUT THE ARCHITECT

Design rooted
in identity.

As a recent architecture graduate, my design approach is rooted in a deep respect for nature, cultural identity, and the human experience. I am especially interested in contextual and nature-based design, creating spaces that respond meaningfully to their environment and community.

My work often draws inspiration from the diverse cultural expressions found throughout Ethiopia, using them as a foundation to shape form, function, and atmosphere. I believe architecture should not only serve but reflect the people and place it belongs to, blending sustainability with cultural narrative.

I bring strong communication skills, fast and efficient software production, and a deep enthusiasm for collaboration and growth. I aspire to become an architect known for thoughtful design that balances ecology, culture, and human need.
